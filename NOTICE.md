## Skill Framework Inspiration

The skill/agent architectural pattern in `rihal/skills/` — the idea of agents with persona files, capability tables, and invocable action skills — is derived from the **BMAD Method** project (MIT licensed).

- **Project:** BMAD Method
- **Source:** https://github.com/bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD
- **License:** MIT
- **Version referenced:** v6.2.2

The operational content (agent personas, skill IDs, workflow language, cultural framing) has been substantially rewritten for Rihal Method. Where BMAD's original content remains structurally similar (task steps, validation patterns), this NOTICE serves as the required MIT attribution.

## Context Management Inspiration

The file-based state approach and context-reset/context-build workflows in `rihal/workflows/` take inspiration from **Get Shit Done (GSD)** by Hanzla Habib (the same author).
